<p>Roblosats is a simple and private app to exchange bitcoin on the BLAKE2b chain for national currencies, peer to peer, over its Lightning network. It is a fork of RoboSats and works the same way: deterministic robot avatars, Lightning hold invoices as escrow and fidelity bonds, PGP-encrypted chat, built-in Tor.</p>
<p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul>
<li>No registration and no KYC: your robot is generated from a token only you keep.</li>
<li>Escrow with Lightning hold invoices: your peer is committed and cannot run away with the funds.</li>
<li>Fidelity bonds: attempts to cheat are penalized.</li>
<li>The buyer can choose to receive on-chain.</li>
<li>Prices from the BLAKE2b chain's own market, in more than 60 fiat currencies.</li>
<li>More than 15 languages.</li>
</ul>
